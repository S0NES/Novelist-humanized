# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Combines humanizer word rules, multi-POV story rules and chinese-novelist chapter craft in one skill, with a clear priority order.
- Planning, writing, revision and pasted-text modes, including unattended scheduled runs that stop for outline approval.
- Outline, character, continuity ledger and chapter templates.
- Language notes for Portuguese, Spanish and Chinese.
- Genre pack for LitRPG and cyberpunk.
- `scripts/wordcount.py` and `scripts/check_tells.py`, with no dependencies.
- `scripts/build-release.py` and a Release workflow that attaches the skill zip to each tagged release.
