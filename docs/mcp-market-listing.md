## Long description

Humanized Novelist plans a book with you, then writes and revises it one chapter at a time. Every sentence follows humanizer-based word rules, so the usual AI tells don't appear: "not X but Y" contrasts, one-line closers, stock idioms, dashes everywhere, and characters answering "You're cruel" with "I'm not cruel, I'm precise."

It keeps an outline with a TODO list and chapter summaries, character sheets, and a continuity ledger, so long serials stay consistent across hundreds of chapters. Each chapter opens with a hook in its first fifth and ends on one of ten closing hooks. A second pass revises the chapter against the same rules before it is saved.

It works in any language, with notes for Portuguese, Spanish and Chinese, and runs unattended on a schedule. An optional LitRPG and cyberpunk genre pack adds system rules and makes every upgrade cost something. Two dependency-free scripts count chapter length and scan for surface AI tells.
