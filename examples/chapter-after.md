# Chapter 7: The Lower Ring

## Working notes
- Core event: Kael and Mira break into the maintenance level to find the missing courier.
- Answers the previous hook: the courier's tracker died on this floor.
- Closing hook: the courier's implant is still running, but not in the courier.
- POV: Kael

## Text

Kael counted the doors instead of breathing. Four on the left, three on the right, and the fourth on the left had a fresh scratch beside the lock, bright metal under the grime.

"You're reckless," Mira said.

He was already kneeling. "Hold the light still."

"I'm holding it still. Your hands are the ones shaking."

They were. The left implant had been running hot since the stairwell, a dull ache from wrist to elbow that the clinic upstairs had promised would settle in a week. That was six weeks and two payments ago. He pressed the shim into the lock and felt the pins drag.

"If the courier's in there," Mira said, "and he's dead, we walk away. We don't touch anything. We tell Oduya the tracker was right and we go home."

"And if he's alive?"

She didn't answer. The light moved half a centimeter and came back.

The lock gave on the third try. Inside, the room smelled of solder and old coffee. A cot, a folding table, a jacket on a hook with the courier company's yellow stripe on the sleeve. No courier.

Mira swept the light across the table and stopped. A clear tray held a courier-grade implant, the kind they put in the forearm so the company can find its packages. Its status diode blinked green.

"That's his," Kael said. "Serial matches."

"It's still on."

"They run three days on body heat." He looked at the empty cot. "Then it should be dead."

The diode kept blinking. Under the tray, a thin cable ran down through a hole drilled in the table and into the floor, toward the level the maps said was solid concrete.
