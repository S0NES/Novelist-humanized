## chinese-novelist

The chapter craft in `references/chapter-craft.md`, `references/dialogue.md`, `references/characters.md` and `references/templates.md` is translated and adapted from **chinese-novelist 1.0.0**, a Chinese-language novel-writing skill distributed on MCP Market. The author and license of the original package are not stated in the copy used here. If you are its author, please open an issue so the credit can name you.

## Story rules

The story rules in `SKILL.md` section 3 and `references/genres/litrpg-cyberpunk.md` come from **narrativa-multi-pov-regras** by Vinicius de Souza Scarabelli.
