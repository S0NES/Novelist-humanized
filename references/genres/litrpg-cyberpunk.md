# Genre pack: LitRPG and cyberpunk

Load this when the outline names `litrpg-cyberpunk`. It adds to the story rules in `SKILL.md`.

## System grammar (LitRPG)

- The status interface has one fixed format for the whole book. Define it in `world.md` or the outline.
- It appears in at most one scene every three chapters, and only when the change matters to the plot.
- Every upgrade has a visible cost and is earned on the page. No upgrade solves a scene by itself.
- When a rule fails (an implant misfires, a tool behaves differently), the text acknowledges the failure and characters speculate about why. Never rewrite a rule in silence. Log the failure in the ledger.
- Mechanical danger treated seriously in one chapter does not become a joke right after without the text noticing. Comedy and danger can share a scene if the choice is visible.

## Cyberpunk grammar

- Technology is a tool with a price: battery, debt, rejection, license, surveillance.
- No magic words, even disguised. "Energy" with no technical basis, "aura" and "attunement" are out.
- Inequality shows in language: who speaks how, on which floor, with which implant.
- Explain the city through cost and use: who pays, who watches, what breaks.

## Arcs

- Action arcs may introduce new names often.
- Bottle arcs (closed setting, small cast) deepen the characters who already exist.

## Ledger additions

Track implants, their maintenance and rejection, debts and who holds them, licenses, and every system stat that changed, with the chapter where it changed.

## Checklist additions

- No upgrade without a cost.
- No silent rule change.
- No magic terms.
- Status interface used at most once in three chapters.
